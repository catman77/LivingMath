# Scope and limitations

Developer preview, single owner on Linux. Assurance/Semantic Bridge previously tested on Python3.13/3.14; the added White Star dependency profile is tested on Python3.13 Linux x86_64 with Landlock ABI≥3 and C++17. Product v0.3 preserves assurance v0.1, Semantic Bridge v0.2 and legacy observer012 protocol1.2.0rc3. This is finite/source assurance and bounded numerical exploration. Native SUTI NOT_EXECUTED; Lean/theorem-prover adapter UNAVAILABLE_NOT_REGISTERED; no PROVED_FORMAL or verified admission.

Assurance task drafting is explicit structured import and review. Semantic generation uses the existing owner-authenticated Codex backend with tools disabled; failures are retained as MODEL_UNAVAILABLE, without fixture substitution. It consumes the owner's existing model entitlement and may be unavailable. A private receipt spool has a 16-attempt ceiling per project; it is not a throughput benchmark. Arbitrary schema/program execution, external references in checks, multiuser operation, billing, model training and new benchmarks are out of scope. Common PII/token checks cannot establish comprehensive de-identification. Source rights are declarations, not legal determinations.

Literary claims remain UNKNOWN/HOLD. SOURCE_ATTESTED confirms exact quote-envelope bytes after independent source preflight, not authorial intent or prose truth. Structural loss covers declared annotation fields and an explicitly enumerated finite Q/domain; differences in words do not certify differences in the physical world. Replay checks saved provenance and deterministic contracts, not literary accuracy, provider authenticity or the completeness of a reading. Publication restrictions apply to prompts and derived quotations as well as raw sources.

Automated clean-room and HTTPS/OAuth acceptance cannot substitute two external technical people. R3 remains pending until real independent replay reports exist. A working installed preview does not make the final R3/R4 release gate PASS.

White Star coordinates and axes are explicit encoding assumptions. The supplied unchanged algorithm explores only k candidates per step, preserves all aliases and excludes zero-direction readings; numerical novelty is not literary value. The comparison's `suti` engine identifies the actual frozen-input Bridge/assurance facade, not a native SUTI runtime or the entire legacy DAV pipeline. It checks declared annotation bytes and presents the first K declared concepts for the caller's interpretation. All common concepts/relations remain in the decoder packet; alternative frame selection is a separate declared projection choice.

The three rational model families check concrete1..8 dimensional instances (cyclic_order2x2 only). A good matrix does not certify the literary mapping, a general theorem or a physical model. Historical interruption/limit receipts are retained; replay regenerates completed computations, not elapsed historical failures. Run input/protocol and actual summary expose requested/effective budgets; no silent budget reduction is performed. Caller model provenance and evaluation are declarations, not provider-signed or independent human certification.
