# Scope and limitations

Developer preview, single owner on Linux; Python3.13/3.14 tested. The product version0.1 is separate from retained legacy observer012 protocol1.2.0rc3. This is finite/source assurance, not a universal truth engine. Native SUTI NOT_EXECUTED; Lean/formal adapter UNAVAILABLE_NOT_REGISTERED; no PROVED_FORMAL or verified admission.

Task drafting is explicit structured import and review. Hosted generation is optional and not implemented here. Arbitrary schema/program execution, external references, multiuser operation, billing, model training and new benchmarks are out of scope. Common PII/token checks cannot establish comprehensive de-identification. Source rights are declarations, not legal determinations.

Automated clean-room and HTTPS/OAuth acceptance cannot substitute two external technical people. R3 remains pending until real independent replay reports exist. A working installed preview does not make the final R3/R4 release gate PASS.
